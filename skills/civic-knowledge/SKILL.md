# Civic Knowledge

1. Identify the jurisdiction, service, program, or public process involved.
2. Explain the process in plain language and distinguish general guidance from verified local facts.
3. Note dates, eligibility rules, contact details, or policies that may have changed and need confirmation.
4. Help the user prepare a short list of questions or documents for the responsible office.
5. Do not present the agent as a government office or as legal, medical, or emergency advice.
