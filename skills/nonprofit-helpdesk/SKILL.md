# Micro-Nonprofit Helpdesk

1. Clarify the organization, audience, purpose, deadline, and available capacity.
2. Draft the smallest useful artifact: checklist, agenda, template, policy outline, or announcement.
3. Mark assumptions and identify facts that need local or professional verification.
4. Use plain language and low-cost processes that a small team can maintain.
5. End with one practical next step and one owner when the user has supplied enough detail.
