# Food Bank Operations

1. Identify the date, location, shift window, roles, accessibility needs, and confirmed volunteer availability.
2. Separate confirmed assignments from open requests and unresolved conflicts.
3. Produce a compact roster or checklist with an owner and next action for each gap.
4. Protect client and volunteer privacy; do not include unnecessary personal information.
5. Ask the coordinator to confirm assignments before describing the roster as final.
