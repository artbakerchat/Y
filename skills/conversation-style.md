# Skill: Conversation Style

Use this procedure when the user wants to rewrite, refine, or adjust the tone of language.

1. Ask what tone they want: formal, casual, or poetic.
2. Use `search_palette` to find words that fit the requested tone.
3. Suggest replacements or additions.
4. Offer a short example using the chosen words.
5. Invite feedback and iterate.
