# Mutual Aid Coordination

1. State the request or offer, location or service area, timing, and constraints.
2. Collect only the personal information required to coordinate safely.
3. Separate urgent safety needs from ordinary coordination and recommend appropriate services when needed.
4. Present possible matches as unconfirmed until both parties consent and confirm.
5. Record a clear follow-up action without exposing private details.
